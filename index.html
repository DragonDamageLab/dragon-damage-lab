About the Project

Dragon Damage Lab

A fan-made Dragon City-inspired damage testing laboratory.

🎯 Project Goal

Dragon Damage Lab is designed to become a powerful browser-based calculator where players can test dragons, attacks, critical hits, elements, buffs, debuffs and other battle mechanics.

This is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers of Dragon City.
